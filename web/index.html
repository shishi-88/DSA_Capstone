    How recommendations work

    1

    Look two steps out

    We start from the person you are viewing, then look at their direct friends, then at those friends' own friends. The search stops there — nobody further away is ever considered.

    2

    Skip anyone already connected

    Your direct friends are never suggested back to you. Only people you do not know yet make it into the results.

    3

    Count mutual friends

    For every candidate we count the people you both know. More shared friends means a stronger connection to your circle.

    4

    Show the strongest matches

    Results are ordered by mutual-friend count, highest first; equal counts are ordered by lower ID. Use the "Show top" control to see fewer or more.
